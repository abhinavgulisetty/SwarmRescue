What these clips demonstrate

The robot moves through wheel actuators. Local survivor detection uses range and wall visibility. The detour is calculated by the native integer routing kernel after the fallen geometry is scanned. The survivor follows under bounded forces; evacuation requires crossing the outdoor boundary.

The search site is supplied and debris release is deliberately timed for teaching. A complete structural map is available to the planner. The survivor is an idealized ambulatory capsule. These clips explain individual components; the separate full mission demonstrates the swarm.
